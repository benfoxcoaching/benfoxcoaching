Three conversations, in order

Something has to change.

You're good at your job. Maybe great at it. And something still feels off.

I've talked about that feeling on a lot of podcasts. These are the three I'd send a friend who's stuck. Listen in order: they go from noticing the problem, to understanding it, to deciding what to do about it.

  1. 1
    Modern CTO

    Why high-achieving engineers still feel something's missing

    Start here if everything looks great on paper and you can't figure out why you feel this way. We talk about why a bigger title won't fix it, why you shouldn't look for the next job while you're running on empty, and how a principal engineer who'd already quit figured out what he wanted and became a CTO with time for school pickup.

    Short on time? Start at 17:21, then skip to 32:36 for his story.

  2. 2
    The Impactful Engineer

    Before you quit your job, figure out what you're actually running from

    Listen to this one if you can't tell whether the job is the problem or you are. We get into bad managers, weak boundaries, and why high performers carry the same habits into the next job if they don't deal with them first.

  3. 3
    Back After Burnout

    Setting your quit date

    Listen to this one if leaving feels terrifying, even though you have options. We talk about picking a quit date, getting honest about your financial runway, and why you only need the next step, not the next ten.

    Short on time? Skip to 9:22 for the quit date.

If one of these hit close to home, let's talk.
